## 23.02.2026

After our daily meeting my whole day was focused on designing a global scene of the robot and all the supports needed :

- For elevating the duck
- For holding the tool that will color the duck (for now it's acrylic felt but it can changed based on our result on test and the efficiency to paint a surface)

For the duck support, I think the final design will not differ so much of what I have done until now and last week. However, I explored different idea for the tools' support :

- A support to hold the plug of the pen but the robot could put the felt back in its plug without being able to pull it out without our help so it was, at this moment, not the solution chosen.

- A support that fit the shape of a pen but the disadvantage is that it is not modular. If I chose this option I would need to design a support every time we decide to change the tool.

But in the end, I design a cone-shaped support with a hole in the middle to give space to the tip of the pen. Usually, pen, pencil and so are designed to be pointy at the end. That is why I choosed this design.

My global idea consists to design 3 main pieces : as I said a support for duck and tools but also (if possible, it was in discussion with Pr. Darbellay) a wooden piece that will be the base to fit the supports but also to take the shape of the environement.

I booked an appointment with Pr. Darbellay to talk about how can I asked him to form a wooden piece and what do I have to give to him. I will probably get a response tomorrow.

I also send an e-mail to Pr. Richard to have a first feedback of the job done until now.

## 24.02.2026

The day begins with a twist : our CTO took part of our daily meeting to see how we conduct it and make comment about it. He also speak a bit about the portfolio we had to do.

After the daily meeting, I fixed an appointment with the 3D model validation expert Pr. Richard. It was the occasion to discuss the progress of the sketches and what are the good or bad idea of design.

What I get from this review are theses points :

- About interlocking pieces

> Despite having interlocking pieces, which may cause difficulties about measurement and precisions, We could use pins to align pieces between them. It is less likely to fail because we can adapt the hole extruded in the piece to the pins we have available.

- About duck support

> Having a small base (10mm diameter) is strongly not recommended as the support will inevitably recieved impact. I should have a wider base to ensure durability.

No special comments were made about pen support and so a printing would be done soon.

Sadly I didn't manage to meet Pr. Darbellay to talk about wooden pieces.
